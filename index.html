Science of Purity

The 9-Stage L'VURA Purification Cycle

Our proprietary multi-barrier refining system eliminates impurities at molecular resolution while conserving crucial health minerals.

01

Multi-Depth Sand Filtration

Removes suspended sediment, organic colloids, and particulate micro-debris down to 10 microns through pressurized volcanic quartz sand.

02

Activated Carbon Bed

Adsorbs residual chlorine, organic hydrocarbons, and any lingering aroma or flavor to produce a crisp, neutral, silken palate.

03

Reverse Osmosis & Nano Filter

Semi-permeable TFC membranes strip away heavy metals, dissolved chemical contaminants, and micro-plastics at 0.0001 microns.

04

Mineral Remapping & UV

Carefully recalibrated doses of food-grade Magnesium and Calcium are introduced, followed by dual-spectrum germicidal UV-C stabilization.
